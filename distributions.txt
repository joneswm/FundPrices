# Distribution schedules for instruments no source covers (SPEC-010, #85).
#
# Yahoo supplies the dividends of the YA instruments in funds.txt. The OEIC
# share classes priced from the Financial Times have no such source, but their
# ex-dividend (XD) and payment dates are fixed by the fund and printed on its
# factsheet. A line here turns that schedule into estimated rows in
# data/dividends_upcoming.csv and, when the amounts carry an as-of date, into
# past rows in data/dividends_history.csv with Source "config".
#
# Format: <identifier>,<frequency>,<xd-dates>,<pay-offset-days>[,<last-amounts>[,<as-of>]]
#
#   identifier       an identifier published in funds.txt (lookup id or alias) or
#                    an identifier in closed_holdings.txt
#   frequency        monthly, quarterly, semiannual or annual
#   xd-dates         ;-separated MM-DD ex-dividend dates, one per payment in a
#                    year (12, 4, 2 or 1); 02-29 means 28 February in a common year
#   pay-offset-days  days from each ex-date to its payment (0 to 365)
#   last-amounts     optional; ;-separated gross amounts per share, one per
#                    xd-date in the same order, empty for "not known"; in the
#                    unit the identifier's price is quoted in (GBP, not pence,
#                    for a fund priced in GBP)
#   as-of            optional YYYY-MM-DD, only with last-amounts: the date the
#                    amounts were checked against the fund. Amounts without it
#                    feed estimates only; with it, the latest occurrence of
#                    each ex-date on or before it is also written to the history
#
# Update the amounts and the as-of date after each payment: an old amount is
# projected forward, not noticed. Example (not a real fund):
#
# XX0000000000,semiannual,03-01;09-01,30,0.0821;0.0794,2026-10-01
